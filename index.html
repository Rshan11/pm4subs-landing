FAQs

?
Question

What is PM4Subs?

PM4Subs is project management software built specifically for subcontractors. It helps you track bids, jobs, change orders, specs, field tasks, and tools in one place so you spend less time chasing information and more time running work.

?
Question

Is PM4Subs only for masonry contractors?

No. PM4Subs works for any trade — masonry, concrete, framing, drywall, steel, and more. It was designed by a masonry subcontractor, but the workflows fit most trades.

?
Question

How is PM4Subs different from Procore?

Procore is built for general contractors. It's powerful, expensive, and designed around owner meetings and drawing logs. PM4Subs focuses on what subcontractors actually need: change order documentation, field task tracking, spec review, and staying organized without a full-time office staff.

?
Question

Can PM4Subs help with specs and submittals?

Yes. The Spec Analyzer is built for subcontractors who get handed 2,000-page spec books. Upload the PDF and pull out the sections that matter for your trade — materials, accessories, submittal requirements — without reading the whole thing.

?
Question

Does PM4Subs replace my accounting software?

No. PM4Subs works alongside your existing accounting system. It organizes jobs, tasks, specs, and field documentation so your billing is cleaner and easier — but it's not an accounting platform.

?
Question

Can my foremen use PM4Subs on their phones?

Yes. PM4Subs is built mobile-first so foremen can see job tasks, upload photos for change order documentation, and update the office from the field without fighting a complicated app.
